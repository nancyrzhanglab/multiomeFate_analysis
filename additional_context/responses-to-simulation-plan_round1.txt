* In general: The lineage-DE suffers since there's only one or two lineage that is "high" (so all the genes are more indicative of that one lineage than it is of the true underlying mechanics -- in general, when the clonal expansion process gets complicated, lineage-DE will suffer since it needs to make a "cutoff" to stratify the groups, and also suffers since it needs to combine all the high-expansion lineages together into one group), and CoSPAR will suffer since the gene expression of the high-expansion lineage at the later timepoint might not be anywhere near the gene expression of that same lineage at the earlier timepoint. (I.e., CoSPAR in general suffers since it needs to rely on a very smooth continuum between the two timepoints, which doesn't need be true in our setting).
